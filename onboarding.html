That's the whole idea.

On a real application, open DraftBack and choose Protect this site. Approve website access, then fill the form normally. Wait for Saved locally. After a reload, choose Restore my answers and review the result.

Enable protection before typing. DraftBack cannot recover answers lost before it was enabled. Keep using the same tab and application URL. This does not restore your login, upload files or submit anything.

This walkthrough uses the real capture-and-restore engine with separate, session-only practice storage. Sample answers never become your autofill profile.

What is and isn't protected?

Supported short and long text, dates, native dropdowns, radio choices and ordinary checkboxes. Security/payment fields, identity numbers, uploads and legal consent are excluded. Custom dropdowns, closed shadow roots and cross-origin embedded forms may be unsupported. Protection is temporary (24 hours), not a backup. A crash before a save is acknowledged may lose the last edit.

For another website's embedded form, open it directly and enable protection there. If the application URL changes or the browser loses the tab's session data, automatic matching may not be available.
